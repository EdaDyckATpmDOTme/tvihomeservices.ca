About TVI Home Services

A family business built on helping people, building community, and making Vancouver Island more beautiful.

Our Story

TVI Home Services was founded in 2021 by Herman and Shannon Smelt as a division of Trimlight Van Isle Inc. What began with a passion for permanent, programmable LED lighting quickly grew into a full-service exterior property care company.

Based in the Cowichan Valley, we now serve homeowners and businesses across all of Vancouver Island — from Sooke and Sidney in the south to Campbell River in the north, including the Gulf Islands.

Our company was built on a simple belief: helping people take pride in their homes should be accessible, professional, and hassle-free. Whether it’s crystal-clear windows, a moss-free roof, gleaming siding, or stunning permanent lighting, we bring the same care and attention to every job.

Today, TVI Home Services offers a full range of exterior cleaning and restoration services alongside our Trimlight lighting systems. We added tile work to our lineup to bring the same quality craftsmanship indoors.

Part of the Trimlight Van Isle Family

TVI Home Services operates as a division of Trimlight Van Isle Inc., one of Vancouver Island’s leading providers of permanent, programmable LED lighting systems. Through our parent company, we offer a lifetime product warranty on all Trimlight lighting installations, plus a 5-year local service warranty for Vancouver Island customers.

Our lighting systems are installed by trained technicians and controlled through an app, giving you complete control over colours, patterns, and schedules — no ladders required, no seasonal hassle.

Visit trimlightvanisle.ca to learn more about our lighting services.
