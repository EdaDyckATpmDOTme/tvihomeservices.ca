Window Washing

Crystal-clear windows for homes and businesses across Vancouver Island. Professional results, no ladders required on your end.

Keep Your Windows Sparkling in Coastal Conditions

Vancouver Island’s coastal environment is beautiful, but it takes a toll on your windows. Salt spray, tree pollen, rainwater spotting, dirt, debris, spider webs, and insects all build up faster here than inland. Regular professional cleaning helps guard against that buildup and keeps your home looking its best.

Our crew handles all the ladders and extended-reach equipment so you don’t have to climb or reach second-storey glass yourself. We deliver a streak-free finish every time.

Benefits of Professional Window Washing

Regular Maintenance

Routine cleaning limits the buildup of salt, moisture, and environmental contaminants that accumulate faster in coastal conditions.

Curb Appeal

Clear, sparkling windows help your property look well maintained and inviting. More natural light improves your interior spaces too.

Window Health

Removing grime, hard water stains, and coastal residue can extend the lifespan of your windows and prevent etching damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have my windows cleaned?

Most homes benefit from twice-yearly cleaning. However, on Vancouver Island we recommend every 3 to 4 months because of salt air, rain, and faster buildup in our coastal climate.

Is professional window washing worth it?

Absolutely. You get streak-free results, more natural light in your home, and longer window life. We handle all the equipment and heights so you stay safe on the ground.

Why is window cleaning different on the Island?

Coastal conditions cause faster buildup of salt spray, moisture, pollen, and debris than inland areas. Windows on Vancouver Island need more frequent attention to stay clean and prevent long-term damage.

Do I need to be home during the service?

Not always. Our crew needs access to the areas being cleaned, and we ask that screens be removed in advance. As long as we can reach the windows, you don’t need to be there.
