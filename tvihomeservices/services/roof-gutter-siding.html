Why Island Homes Need Regular Exterior Care

Vancouver Island’s combination of heavy rainfall, coastal humidity, and tree canopy creates conditions where moss, algae, and mildew grow faster than most other regions in Canada. Left alone, these organisms cause real structural damage: moss lifts shingles, clogged gutters overflow into foundations, and algae stains eat into siding.

Regular maintenance prevents costly repairs. A clean roof lasts longer, gutters that drain properly protect your foundation, and siding free of organic growth stays in better condition. We recommend annual roof and gutter service, with siding soft washing every one to two years depending on exposure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Won’t pressure washing damage my roof?

We never use high pressure on roofs. Our team removes moss manually and applies treatments that kill remaining growth without damaging shingles or tiles.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

At least twice a year—once in late fall after leaves drop, and again in spring. Homes near heavy tree cover may need a third cleaning mid-summer.

What’s the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure with cleaning solutions to kill and remove organic growth. Pressure washing uses high water pressure to blast off dirt. Soft washing is safer for siding, paint, and delicate surfaces.

Can you bundle roof, gutter, and siding work?

Yes. We often do all three in a single visit, which saves time and reduces the total cost. Contact us for a bundled quote.
