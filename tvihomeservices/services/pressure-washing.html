Fight Back Against Island Weather

Vancouver Island’s damp climate encourages moss, algae, dirt, and grime to build up on outdoor surfaces faster than most places in Canada. Left unchecked, these contaminants create slippery hazards, damage materials, and make your property look neglected.

Our pressure washing service covers driveways, walkways, decks, patios, and other hard surfaces. We use appropriate pressure levels for each material and apply soft washing techniques where needed to protect delicate surfaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I pressure wash?

Most homes benefit from about once a year. On Vancouver Island, we recommend every 6 to 12 months to manage moss, algae, and moisture buildup from our damp coastal climate.

Which surfaces can be pressure washed?

Driveways, siding, patios, decks, and walkways. We adjust pressure levels for each material and use soft washing where high pressure could cause damage.

Is pressure washing safe for all surfaces?

Yes, when done professionally. Our team uses proper equipment and techniques, including soft washing for surfaces that need gentler treatment. We match the pressure to the material.

How do I prepare for the service?

Move outdoor furniture away from the work area, close windows, and make sure we have clear access to the surfaces being cleaned. We handle the rest.

Why is pressure washing important on the Island?

Damp coastal conditions encourage moss, algae, and slipping hazards faster than inland areas. Regular pressure washing keeps your surfaces safe and prevents long-term material damage.
