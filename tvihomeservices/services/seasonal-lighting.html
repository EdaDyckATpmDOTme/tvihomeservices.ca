Frequently Asked Questions

When should I book holiday lighting?

Book early—ideally by September or October. The installation window fills quickly, and early booking ensures your display is up before December.

What’s the difference between seasonal and permanent lighting?

Seasonal lighting goes up for a specific period (usually holidays) and comes down after. Permanent LED systems like Trimlight are installed once, stay mounted year-round, and can be controlled via an app to display any color or pattern for any occasion.

Do you do commercial lighting?

Yes. We handle commercial properties of all sizes, from storefronts to sports facilities. Ask about our portfolio of commercial projects.
