Quality Tile Work for Your Home

Whether you’re renovating a bathroom, upgrading a kitchen backsplash, or refreshing a tired entryway, professional tile work transforms your space. TVI Home Services now offers full tile installation, repair, and grouting services across Vancouver Island.

We work with all common tile types—ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, glass, and mosaic—and handle everything from surface preparation to the final grout and caulk.

Our Process

1

Surface Preparation

We ensure the substrate is clean, level, and properly prepared. This includes membrane installation for wet areas and backer board where needed.

2

Layout & Dry Fit

Tiles are laid out dry to confirm the pattern, check cuts, and ensure a balanced, professional appearance before any adhesive goes down.

3

Cutting & Installation

Precision cutting for edges, corners, and fixtures. Tiles are set with proper adhesive and spacing for a clean, uniform result.

4

Grouting & Caulking

Joints are grouted and cleaned. Perimeter joints and wet-area transitions receive flexible caulk for waterproof sealing.

5

Final Inspection & Cleanup

We inspect every detail, clean all surfaces, and walk you through the finished work. Your space is left ready to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of tile do you work with?

We install ceramic, porcelain, natural stone (marble, slate, travertine), glass, and mosaic tiles. We can advise on the best choice for your project based on location, budget, and style.

Can you replace individual broken tiles?

Yes. We can remove and replace cracked or damaged tiles without disturbing the surrounding work, as long as matching or complementary tile is available.

Do you tile showers and wet areas?

Absolutely. We install proper waterproofing membranes and use appropriate materials for shower floors, walls, niches, and tub surrounds to ensure long-term durability.

How long does a backsplash take?

A standard kitchen backsplash typically takes 1–2 days including preparation, installation, and grouting. Larger or more complex patterns may take longer.

Can you fix grout without replacing the tile?

Yes. Grout repair and re-grouting are common services. We remove deteriorated grout, clean the joints, and apply fresh grout to restore both appearance and waterproofing.
