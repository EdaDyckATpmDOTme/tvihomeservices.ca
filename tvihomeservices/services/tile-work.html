Frequently Asked Questions

What types of tile do you work with?

Ceramic, porcelain, natural stone (marble, slate, travertine), glass mosaic, and large-format tiles. We can advise on the best material for your space and budget.

Do you supply the tile or do I need to buy it?

Either way works. We can help you select and source tile, or we’re happy to install tile you’ve already purchased. Just make sure you have enough for cuts and waste—we can advise on quantities.

How long does a typical tile job take?

A backsplash usually takes one to two days. A full bathroom retile runs three to five days depending on size and prep work needed. We’ll give you a timeline with your quote.

Can you fix just a few cracked tiles?

Yes. We do single-tile replacements and small repairs. If matching tile is no longer available, we can suggest alternatives that blend with your existing installation.
