Frequently Asked Questions

What is Trimlight deck and patio lighting?

A permanent, professionally installed LED system with customizable colours for year-round use on your outdoor living spaces.

Can it be used on decks and patios?

Yes, including pergolas and other outdoor living areas.

How does permanent patio lighting work?

LED fixtures connect to a controller that adjusts colour, brightness, and schedules by app or remote.

Does it improve outdoor living spaces?

Yes. It makes decks and patios comfortable and usable after dark.

Is it safe?

Yes. It uses low-voltage, professionally installed systems suited to areas near railings, stairs, and seating.

Can I change colours for events and seasons?

Yes. Set colours for holidays, gatherings, or everyday ambience.

Does it increase deck safety?

Yes. Better lighting reduces trip hazards and improves visibility.

Are the lights weather-resistant?

Yes. They are built for rain, snow, and temperature changes.

Will it increase energy costs?

No. LED lights use little power and have low operating costs.

Why choose Trimlight?

A low-maintenance, permanent solution that improves appearance, safety, and control.
