Our Lighting Services

From subtle architectural accents to vibrant holiday displays, we design and install lighting solutions tailored to your property.

Residential Lighting

Permanent LED lighting that transforms your home. Accent your rooflines, eaves, and architectural features with customizable colours and patterns controlled from your phone.

Landscape Lighting

Illuminate your gardens, pathways, and outdoor features. Add landscape lighting to an existing Trimlight system or as a standalone installation to enhance your property after dark.

Ridge Lighting

Highlight the peaks and ridgelines of your roof with clean, elegant LED lighting. Ridge lighting adds a dramatic silhouette effect that makes your home stand out day or night.

Down Lighting

Surface-mounted accent lights that cast a warm glow downward along walls and surfaces. Perfect for creating depth, ambiance, and improved visibility around your property.

Deck & Patio Lighting

Extend your outdoor living space into the evening. Our deck and patio lighting creates the perfect atmosphere for entertaining, relaxing, or enjoying a quiet Vancouver Island sunset.

Commercial Lighting

Make your business stand out with permanent LED lighting. Attract customers, enhance your brand presence, and improve security with professional commercial installations.
