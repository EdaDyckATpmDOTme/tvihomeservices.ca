Our Story

About Trimlight Van Isle

A family business built on a love of helping people and strengthening community on Vancouver Island since 2021.

How It All Started

Trimlight Van Isle Inc. was founded in 2021 by Herman and Shannon Smelt. What began as a shared passion for helping people and building community has grown into Vancouver Island's trusted name in permanent LED lighting.

Starting with programmable lighting systems, the company has expanded into residential, commercial, landscape, temporary, and custom property lighting. Every installation is designed to be attractive, long-lasting, and tailored to each client's vision.

Based in the Cowichan Valley, we serve families and businesses across Vancouver Island, from Ladysmith to Sooke, Sidney to Nanaimo, and the Gulf Islands. Our mission is simple: help Vancouver Island families and businesses mark life's moments with safe, low-effort permanent lighting, while strengthening local communities.

Herman & Shannon Smelt — Founders, Trimlight Van Isle
